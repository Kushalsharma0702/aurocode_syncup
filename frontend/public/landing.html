The problem

Feedback arrives broken. Then you pay to reassemble it.

Every project that ships visual work for a non-technical buyer loses hours to the same three things.

Translation loss

A client describes a problem in words. You spend real time working out which page, which element, which state. Every screenshot is a small decoding job.

"The thing at the top looks squished on my phone"

Scattered everywhere

Feedback forks the moment two people reply. Nothing is anchored to the live page, so version 3 arrives through a different channel than version 2 — and gets missed.

WhatsApp · Email · Voice note · screenshot.png

Status anxiety

The client can't see progress, so they ask. Asking costs you a context switch; not answering costs them confidence. Nobody enjoys the Friday update email.

"Just checking in — any update on this?"
How it works

One loop, and it closes where the client can see it

Feedback tools capture well but go quiet afterwards. Client portals have nothing happening inside them. SyncUp is the loop that joins the two.

01

They point

Your client clicks the element that's wrong on their own live site and types one sentence. A screenshot, the page URL and the click position attach automatically.

Client · 10 seconds
02

It becomes work

It arrives in your board as a real task, tagged as feedback — not an email you retype. You're notified; they're told when to expect an update.

SyncUp · instant
03

You fix it

Work it like any other task. When you mark it done, the client is told it's fixed and live, with a link straight back to the thing they pointed at.

You · in your own time
04

They confirm

The client checks and taps Looks fixed — or reopens it if it isn't right. The loop closes in front of them, which is the part that builds trust.

Client · one tap

What's in the box

A client portal with something actually happening inside it

The loop is the point. Everything else exists to support it.

📌

Point-and-click feedback

One script on the client's site. They click the element, type a line, and it's filed with a screenshot.

🔗

No-login access

Magic links instead of passwords, shareable over WhatsApp in one tap. Revocable whenever you want.

🔁

Closed-loop status

Clients see their own item move to Fixed, then confirm it or reopen it. No more silent resolutions.

🛡️

Screenshots that behave

Form values are blanked before capture, images served from your own domain, keys locked to your client's site.

Proposals & tasks

📈

Honest progress
